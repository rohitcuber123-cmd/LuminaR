# 68. Academic Paper Title Options and Venue Alignment

## 1. Overview and Title Design Strategy

An academic systems title must be precise, avoid hyperbolic marketing buzzwords ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking", "all-in-one"), accurately reflect the paper's core contributions, and align with the expectations of the target publication venue.

In accordance with the recommended primary narrative (**Narrative F: Multi-Library Platform with Grounded Knowledge Tools**, established in [67_PAPER_FOCUS_OPTIONS.md](file:///d:/SDC/docs/research_evidence/67_PAPER_FOCUS_OPTIONS.md)), title candidates are organized across four venue profiles:
1. **Digital Libraries & Information Systems (Primary Venue Profile)**
2. **Information Retrieval & Knowledge Discovery Profile**
3. **Applied Systems & Software Engineering Profile**
4. **Educational Technology & Learning Analytics Profile**

---

## 2. Title Candidates by Venue Profile

### Profile 1: Digital Libraries (e.g., ACM/IEEE JCDL, Information Processing & Management, D-Lib)

- **Option 1.1 (Top Recommended for JCDL)**:
  > **LuminaR: An Intelligent Multi-Library Platform Integrating Tenant-Isolated Semantic Retrieval and Grounded Knowledge Synthesis**
  - *Strengths*: Directly states the system name, domain (multi-library), retrieval paradigm (tenant-isolated semantic), and generation paradigm (grounded knowledge synthesis). Extremely scholarly and well-balanced.
  - *Target Venues*: ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL), *Information Processing & Management* (Elsevier).

- **Option 1.2**:
  > **Federated Intelligence for Digital Libraries: Tenant-Scoped Hybrid Retrieval and Defensible Generative Tools in LuminaR**
  - *Strengths*: Emphasizes the federated and tenant-scoped security boundaries. Appealing to digital library systems architects.
  - *Target Venues*: JCDL, *Journal of Documentation*.

- **Option 1.3**:
  > **Bridging Catalogue Discovery and Document Comprehension: A Multi-Tenant Digital Library Platform with Constrained RAG**
  - *Strengths*: Highlights the dual-granularity contribution (catalog + full-text document QA).
  - *Target Venues*: *Library Hi Tech*, *International Journal on Digital Libraries* (IJDL).

---

### Profile 2: Information Retrieval & Discovery (e.g., ACM SIGIR, ECIR, ACM CIKM)

- **Option 2.1**:
  > **Tenant-Isolated Hybrid Retrieval and Constrained Local RAG for Institutional Library Networks**
  - *Strengths*: Highly technical; focuses on retrieval mechanics (hybrid dense-lexical, tenant isolation, constrained local generation).
  - *Target Venues*: ACM SIGIR (Short Paper / Demonstration Track), European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR).

- **Option 2.2**:
  > **Dual-Granularity Hybrid Search and Defensive Abstention in Multi-Institutional Retrieval Systems**
  - *Strengths*: Emphasizes IR contributions (dual-granularity search, hybrid ranking, defensive abstention).
  - *Target Venues*: ACM CIKM, *ACM Transactions on Information Systems* (TOIS).

- **Option 2.3**:
  > **LuminaR: Evaluating Multi-Factor Recommendations and Two-Stage Hybrid Retrieval in Constrained Library Environments**
  - *Strengths*: Focuses on empirical evaluation of search and recommendation factors under physical constraints.
  - *Target Venues*: ACM RecSys, ECIR.

---

### Profile 3: Applied Systems & Software Architecture (e.g., IEEE TKDE, IEEE Access, Software: Practice and Experience)

- **Option 3.1 (Top Recommended for IEEE Access / TKDE)**:
  > **Architecture and Empirical Evaluation of LuminaR: A Multi-Tenant Intelligent Middleware for Privacy-Preserving Library Intelligence**
  - *Strengths*: Classic IEEE systems style; highlights architecture, middleware, empirical evaluation, and privacy-preservation.
  - *Target Venues*: *IEEE Access*, *IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering* (TKDE).

- **Option 3.2**:
  > **Deploying Sub-4B Parameter Language Models for Institutional Information Systems: The LuminaR Architecture**
  - *Strengths*: Highlights the practical deployment of small, quantized language models on edge/consumer hardware with defensive guardrails.
  - *Target Venues*: *Software: Practice and Experience* (Wiley), *IEEE Software*.

---

### Profile 4: Educational Technology & Knowledge Systems (e.g., IEEE TLT, AIED)

- **Option 4.1**:
  > **Beyond LLM Hallucinations: Grounded Pedagogical Artifact Generation in Digital Library Systems**
  - *Strengths*: Directly targets the tension between stochastic generative hallucination and deterministic educational tools.
  - *Target Venues*: *IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies* (TLT), International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED).

- **Option 4.2**:
  > **LuminaR: Combining Local RAG Synthesis and Deterministic NLP for Safe Interactive Learning from Institutional Texts**
  - *Strengths*: Explains the dual approach: neural RAG for broad synthesis, deterministic NLP for assessment tools.
  - *Target Venues*: *Computers & Education: Artificial Intelligence*.
