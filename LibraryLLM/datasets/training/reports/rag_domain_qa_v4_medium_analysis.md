## Implication

Most v3 MEDIUM attributes are not safely promotable: the complement is incomplete, the subject is vague, or multiple clauses interfere. A small exact state-change rule may promote a complete named-person state such as 'became pensive'. The spelled-out clock and 'wished to' forms remain MEDIUM under unchanged v2 checks. New v4 coverage must come mainly from independently grounded source patterns, not a blanket MEDIUM promotion.
