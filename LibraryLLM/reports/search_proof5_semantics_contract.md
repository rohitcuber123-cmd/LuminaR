V5 is a disabled engineering candidate derived from the byte-verified Proof V4. Production still uses `search-v2c-c4-deterministic-1`. The authoritative V5 source is `scratch/search_proof_recovery_v5`; `search_proof5_source_freeze.json` records its immutable source snapshot and frozen grammar dependencies. Exact paths, hashes and symbol lines are in `search_proof5_source_map.json`.

V4 assigned advisory status independently in `ownership.front_end` and `proposals.understand`, then imported a second source inventory in `parser.parse_query`. These recognizers used different clause boundaries. A suffix could erase an earlier author; first-person uncertainty could either block an unrelated request or suppress uncertainty about the current request. Missing-slot scanning over a quotation-masked string could consume the blank mask as trailing whitespace and call a supplied quoted language missing. Numeric proof checked isolated conjuncts, losing their owning rating domain. Native edits changed aggregate plan fields while retaining proof of the old source.

V5 introduces `source_roles.partition` before field proposals. It partitions punctuation and independent field/modal conjunctions outside quotation, then classifies speech act, local modality and unresolved association. This is a bounded deterministic language contract, not a general natural-language entailment oracle. In particular it uses lexical and grammatical evidence together; it does not establish general syntactic understanding. Its role rules remain incomplete, as the failed regression assertions demonstrate.

Internal roles cover requested operation, mandatory condition, optional preference, background explanation, quoted literal, example/hypothetical, field/concept reference, explicit exclusion, correction instruction, missing placeholder and unresolved relation/alternative. Internal modalities are MUST, MUST_NOT, SHOULD, MAY, CONDITIONAL, NON_OPERATIVE and UNKNOWN. These are internal representations; no public decision enum or API schema changed. A non-operative inquiry alone cannot authorize an unrequested broad search. An unresolved conditional or association remains blocking. Strong and weak preferences are local to their segment; a relational use of a modal word is not sufficient evidence of advice.

Every `contract.Requirement` retains original query/source/clause offsets, source text, role, modality, field/domain, operator, operand, explicit operand state, mandatory/advisory ownership, polarity, resolution, support, downstream representation and correction provenance. `QueryIR` version 5 retains trusted UI controls separately. Missing, empty, placeholder, ambiguous, contradictory and unsupported are distinct from an absent field. Structured requirements and semantic residue survive into the final decision's `query_ir_contract` and `requirements`. `source_roles` also exposes the segment partition internally. Pure source code imports no gold, benchmark IDs, database, model or production routes.

Source ownership precedes scalar extraction. Non-operative regions are claimed as context; advisory regions as preferences. Quoted bibliographic/language owners consume their complete operands; interior keywords cannot create filters. Numeric domains/operators create a completeness obligation even when their operand is invalid. Numeric negation is dispatched to the comparator domain before generic negative-text recognition. Unknown numeric domains are retained as relationships; invalid supported-domain values do not enter the hard projection. Negative bibliographic and unsupported-domain operations remain blocked. Whole-word exclusion still uses native word boundaries, including Marsh/ar. Contradictory source conditions are retained rather than selecting one operand.

`parser.parse_query` builds a new empty native QueryPlan. Only supplied, supported mandatory IR requirements project into native hard fields. Legacy parsing supplies bounded soft-text/intent proposals only. V5 additionally records semantic residue and verifies the resulting retrieval text. Trusted controls are validated with the native explicit-filter validation and applied separately; user text cannot supply backend identity. Soft/topic omissions are applied after semantic construction. Grammar repair candidates now retain visible provenance through projection.

`proof.validate_ir` checks original spans, clause containment, role/modality consistency, operand states, capability support, negative domain, canonical/grammar repair evidence and native schema. It rebuilds the hard projection from IR and trusted controls, reproduces numeric extraction in the complete repaired owning clause, and checks semantic retrieval text. Source/query, projection or recorded IR digest changes invalidate the proof. `parser.decide` preserves proof metadata across native decision reset. SMART plans constructed without an IR cannot execute. Exact/explicit modes and complete identifier operations retain bounded native delegation.

This proof validates represented source meaning; it cannot prove that every unknown natural-language requirement was extracted. A valid schema/span is insufficient evidence of completeness. The final development failures include real subject-role invention, semantic/technical literal transport, display/intent compatibility and native session transformations. A generic request noun can still become a scoped facet. The fieldless numeric-domain recognizer can also add ambiguity to an already unsupported page-count operation: C5E-R family U03 has five wrong typed decisions, all safely blocked. These are limitations of this frozen revision, not reasons to change normative gold.

Historical strict-field scores remain visible. Perfect execution-decision scores do not imply perfect interpretation, preference encoding, affected-field reporting or ranking. No production-readiness conclusion follows from the exposed development data.
