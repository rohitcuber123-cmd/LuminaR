# Disabled C5E requirement ledger

The ledger is parser evidence, not an authorization capability or a probability. It lives inside the existing decision object; the public schema remains the established six decision kinds, chips, corrections and notice. Production routing and C4 policy are unchanged.

Each request entry contains its field, status, reason, original-query `source_span` (half-open Unicode character offsets), exact `source_text`, normalized value, and `ownership`. MANDATORY and ADVISORY describe user intent. ADVISORY preferences cannot authorize hard filters. RESOLVED means a supported source operand survives composition; a recognized value in a field with unresolved mandatory association becomes UNRESOLVED_RELATIONSHIP. AMBIGUOUS, INCOMPLETE and UNSUPPORTED retain the reason for blocking. Resolved contradictory operands receive CONTRADICTORY when the established validator identifies their conflicting fields. Scope capability validation can annotate `enforcement=CONTEXTUALLY_INAPPLICABLE`; this does not license ignoring a mandatory request. Unsupported format lists also produce the established scope-format decision.

An uncertainty word alone is insufficient to block. The implementation checks field, clause, alternative/association and mandatory ownership evidence. The known ONLINE note cannot be discharged by its nearby ONLINE keyword. Explicit advisory access remains executable without a hard format operand. Missing negative grouping differs from a missing additional subject.

Bounded repairs require a unique one-edit cue with field-specific context, outside opaque quoted literals. Candidate spans are mapped back through the repair offset map. `repair_notices` carry the original changed substring and replacement. Grammar repairs use the existing visible notice field. They do not masquerade as canonical facet correction tokens. Source-owned canonical typo repairs use the existing `{from,to,kind}` correction schema and native issued Apply/Keep choices. Literal interior text is opaque except to an explicit field owner.

Broad quoted whole-word text exclusions stay `exclude_terms`, even when the word is online, digital or physical. Canonical subject exclusions stay `exclude_subjects`. The existing whole-word matching predicate is unchanged: `ar` cannot exclude `Marsh` by substring. No case-ID lookups, benchmark IO, model, database or policy threshold occurs in the candidate.

Candidate 2 additionally consumes source-owned format wrappers with their operand and avoids injecting an artificial `learn` semantic topic when an advisory preference has no residual topic. This repair was driven by exposed C5C development evidence before any independent acceptance inference. Candidate 1, its source and its complete measurements remain in their original C5E namespace.

Limits: the ledger is diagnostic and local to parser construction. It is not a new persistent native workflow protocol. Existing native identity, current membership, version, expiry and one-use reservation checks remain responsible for execution authority. Unknown scalar operands and structured advisory output without an established scoring adapter remain explicitly unscored; they are never converted into invented absence or semantic equality.
