For an illustrative 1.7 billion parameters that is about 3.4 GB at 16 bits or 0.85 GB at 4 bits before quantization metadata, activations, KV cache, buffers, tokenizers and runtime overhead. These are arithmetic planning estimates, not measured allocation or a claim that a billion-parameter model is lightweight. Proposed runtime contract: offline disabled process or bounded local library, input <=500 characters, original text immutable, <=32 requirements and a bounded output/token budget; timeout and memory/queue limits. The extractor may propose operation, source spans, domain, operator, operand, modality, polarity, relation and explicit unknowns. It receives no tenant credentials, database handles or authority, and cannot execute tools. Output must be source-grounded, capability-checked and revalidated by deterministic proof. A timeout, malformed output or uncertain mandatory relation leads to the appropriate clarification/incomplete/unsupported result, never a broader search. Span validity alone cannot prove extraction completeness; adversarial omission/meaning tests remain necessary. EXACT bypasses semantic extraction.

Data plan is a proposal: first use the exposed errors to specify ontology and annotation guidelines, then estimate annotation effort from a small independently reviewed pilot. Source-role/modality/relationship/operand unknowns need explicit span labels and inter-reviewer agreement, not merely decision labels. Do not automatically generate 2,000 contexts. Future development/training and separately sealed structural acceptance populations require separate approval and provenance; the earlier 2,000/500 suggestion is not an approved dataset. Reviewers must be independent and blind to candidate predictions and desired pass rates; distinguish AI review from human review. Existing protected heldout stays untouched.

Before choosing a runtime, measure cold/warm p50/p95, peak RAM/VRAM, serial and bounded concurrency, queue/timeouts and deterministic schema validity on identical source-contract workloads. Compare encoder and constrained-generation variants with a deterministic control, including omission, negation, advisory confusion, anaphora and input injection. Leakage, memorized exposed templates, overconfident unknowns and silent modal loss are principal failure modes.
